Bad Alibi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bad Alibi ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in all Bad Alibi apps that link to this policy and on our website, https://mybadalibi.com.

Information stored in apps

Our apps do not collect personal information or send app progress or usage information to Bad Alibi. Progress and related saved information, such as achievements and preferences, stay in local app storage on your device. We use this local information only to support app features and let you resume your progress.

We do not use app analytics or tracking services to collect information about your use of our apps.

You can delete locally saved information by clearing the app's storage in your device settings. Device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Your operating system may back up and restore app data according to your device and account settings. Backup services are governed by their providers' privacy policies.

Device services

Some apps offer spoken instructions or similar features using your device's text-to-speech service. Text needed for speech is passed to that service, which may be provided by your device manufacturer or another provider. Its processing is governed by that provider's settings and privacy policy. Text-to-speech output does not require our apps to record your voice.

Children's privacy

Some of our apps are intended for children. Bad Alibi does not collect personal information from children through our apps. Saved progress remains on the device, subject to the device's backup settings. Parents or guardians can manage or remove that information through device settings and contact us with privacy questions.

Website information

When you visit our website, limited technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, may be processed by our hosting and analytics services to operate the website and understand visits. These website practices are separate from the app practices described above.

Our website may link to other services, including Amazon. When you follow an external link, that service's privacy policy applies to information it processes.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information or children's personal information.

Support messages are processed by our email provider and retained as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, and meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Purchases and third-party services

App stores process purchases under their own privacy policies. Our apps do not collect or store your payment-card details. Store transaction records and reports are handled according to the store's services and settings.

Operating-system services, backup providers, email services, and website hosting and analytics providers may process information in providing their services. Website and support processing is separate from local app storage.

Sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information processed for our website or support may be handled by service providers for those purposes, or disclosed when required by law.

We take reasonable measures to protect information we hold. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions and backup preferences through your device settings. For apps storing data locally, clearing app storage removes the local saved information; backup copies must be managed separately. Contact us with requests about information held by Bad Alibi or questions about your privacy rights.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our services change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Bad Alibi at bad.alibi@outlook.com.
